## Touch layout and controller layout, upright and sideways (BC, 2026-11-27, 5:36 PM, long-term goal: roadmap G12, 0.9.2 Phone pass, at BC's word 5:38 PM)

- BC: "we need a touchscreen layout and a optimized controller layout for both horizontal and vertical".
- Four layouts, picked by the device in use (kit `input_watch.gd`, G5.14) and the screen shape: touch upright, touch sideways, controller upright, controller sideways. Keyboard and mouse use the touch layout on a laptop or the controller layout, decided on the decision page.
- Touch: big buttons in thumb reach (bottom corners upright, both sides sideways as in P5b), about 48 px targets with gaps, safe margins for notches, rounded corners and the gesture bar.
- Controller: no need for big tap targets, so the fight buttons shrink to slim strips or badge labels (X Play Hand, Y Discard, LB RB Sort) and the freed space goes to bigger cards, the monster and readable charm names. Clear focus highlight on the picked card and slots. Handheld first (Retroid, phone in a clip).
- Switching: change only on a real device change, with a short settle delay so a stray tap does not flip the layout mid-hand. Never move a card under the player's finger or cursor. A Settings option to lock a layout (Auto, Touch, Controller).
- Builds on P5b (buttons beside the hand), P6 (Xbox badges), the sideways panel change and the mobile and small-screen first work. All four layouts get the empty-space rule.
- Next step: a decision page with mock-ups of the four layouts on one fight screen, then the map, reward and town screens.
