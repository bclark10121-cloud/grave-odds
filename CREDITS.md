# Credits

Grave Odds (called Gravehand until 0.4.4) is an original game. The suit shapes, the table and the start screen's motion (candles, embers, the glowing eyes and the lifting card) are drawn in code. The start screen's rose window painting (0.4.4) was made for this game with Google's Nano Banana image models through `tools/nano_banana.py` (G8.8). The monster portraits, court card and Ace pictures, the card back, the map, button, gear and charm medallions and the floor backdrops were made for this game with Google's Nano Banana 2.1 image model (0.4.0 and 0.4.1). The dark gold card face and the suit gems (0.4.3) and the app icon (0.4.2) were made with Google's Nano Banana Pro image model. See `art_src/nb21/README.md`. No personal names here (G1.11).
