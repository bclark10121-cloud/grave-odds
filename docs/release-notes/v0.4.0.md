# Gravehand v0.4.0

The look, part 1. Floor 1 monsters, court cards, Aces and the card back now have stained glass art. All of it was made with Nano Banana 2.1 in Google AI Studio (BC's pick, 2026-11-21).

## Five minute test

1. Start a run on Dusk. The Crypt Rat, Bone Warden or Lantern Wight shows as a stained glass portrait.
2. Turn the phone sideways. The portrait sits at the left at about twice its old size. The name, health and next strike sit beside it.
3. Look at your hand. Jacks, Queens, Kings and Aces show their picture under the big corner rank and suit. Number cards keep a big suit in the middle with a thin gold frame.
4. Find the deck stack at the top right of the info area. It shows the card back and the cards left.
5. Hit a monster. The portrait flashes and shakes.
6. Reach the Chapel Ghoul (elite) and the Ossuary King (boss) on floor 1.

## New in this version

- Five floor 1 monster portraits: Crypt Rat, Bone Warden, Lantern Wight, Chapel Ghoul and Ossuary King.
- Twelve court card pictures: a King, Queen and Jack for each suit.
- Four Ace emblems: a skull spade, a thorned heart, a sword through a diamond and a crowned clover.
- A new card back: a ruby rose window with a crowned skull.
- Readability first: the corner rank and suit are exactly as big as before and always sit on top of the art.
- Sideways fights: the portrait moves to the left and grows from about 100 x 133 to 210 x 280. Upright is unchanged.
- A deck stack with the card back and the count of cards left.
- Card pictures stay smooth at small sizes.
- Tester tools: Fight > Next monster look.

## Look check

- Style matches the references: flat jewel glass, black lead lines, gold trim, candle glow.
- Portrait size by screen: 1280 x 720 shows 210 x 280. 1920 x 1080 shows about 315 x 420. 2400 x 1080 shows about 306 x 408. 540 x 960 shows about 185 x 246.
- Nothing overlaps.
- Upright, the 8 card hand still overlaps (as before). Each court card shows its corner and about half its picture. That is enough to tell them apart.

## Judgment calls

- Art source: BC compared the Gemini app, Nano Banana 2.1 and Nano Banana Pro on one King of Spades prompt and picked Nano Banana 2.1. The Gemini app art made earlier today was redone to match.
- The first Ossuary King prompt (a throne of skulls) was refused. The boss now sits on a dark stone throne in purple robes.
- Card pictures sit in a window from 30 percent down. A small bone notch keeps the corner suit mark clear.
- The crop favors the top of each picture so crowns and faces stay in.
- The deck stack sits at the top right of the info area in both orientations. Sideways the hand fills the full width.
- At 2400 x 1080 there is a wide gap between the portrait and the name column. It reads fine.
- Number cards get no picture. Readability first.
