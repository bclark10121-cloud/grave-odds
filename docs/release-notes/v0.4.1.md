# Gravehand v0.4.1

The look, part 2. The map climbs, the enemy fills the screen and floors 2 and 3 get their stained glass monsters. BC asked for every change here on 2026-11-21.

## Five minute test

1. Start a run. The difficulty page lists Candlelight (easy) first, then Dusk, then Midnight.
2. Look at the map. The first rooms sit at the bottom and the boss sits at the top. Rooms are stained glass medallions. Gold lines show where you may go.
3. Tap a room with a ring, or tap its button at the bottom. Both enter the room.
4. In the fight, look at the enemy. The portrait is much bigger. Its name, health and "Strikes for N" sit on its frame.
5. Pick five cards. The damage panel shows the hand name, Force, Fury and the total as the biggest text on the screen.
6. Turn the device. Sideways the portrait runs from the top bar down to the cards. Upright the monster card fills the middle.
7. Find the gear slots and charm slots. Upright they stand beside the portrait. Sideways they sit in the right column. Tap a filled slot to read it.
8. Look at Play Hand, Discard, Sort and Menu. Each has a medallion icon.
9. Reach floor 2. The Drowned Monk, Ash Hound, Bell Wraith, Iron Penitent and the Tollkeeper have portraits, and the backdrop is the flooded nave.

## New in this version

- The difficulty page shows Candlelight first.
- The map climbs from the bottom to the boss. Six room medallions: Fight, Elite, Treasure, Shrine, Rest and Boss. Lead line paths with gold on the roads open to you. A floor backdrop behind the map.
- Tap a room on the map to enter it. The room buttons stay.
- Ten new monster portraits: Drowned Monk, Ash Hound, Bell Wraith, Iron Penitent, The Tollkeeper, Vault Sexton, Tallow Choir, Gallows Heron, Cinder Reliquary and The Carrion Clock.
- A new fight layout that uses the screen. The monster card holds the name plate, the portrait, the health bar and the strike. A framed damage panel shows the hand. Bosses and elites carry a tag beside the name.
- Bigger portraits. A 4:3 tablet sideways: 395 x 526 (was 307 x 410). The same tablet upright: 335 x 447 (was 257 x 342). A phone upright: 378 x 504 (was 262 x 350). A phone sideways keeps 210 x 280.
- Cards grow on screens with room for them.
- Three floor backdrops behind the fight: the Crypt, the Drowned Nave and the Bell Vaults.
- Gear and charm slots with icons: four gear icons and twenty charm icons. Gear rings show the tier color and tier pips. Tap a filled slot to read the item.
- Medallion icons on Play Hand, Discard, Sort and Menu.

## Look check

- Floors 2 and 3 match the Crypt Rat reference: gold tracery border, pointed arch, jewel panes, black lead lines.
- The room, button, gear and charm medallions share one gold rim and one glass style.
- Nothing overlaps at the six sizes.
- Passed rooms on the map are dim on purpose. They read as "behind you".

## Judgment calls

- All 0.4.1 art came from Nano Banana 2.1 in a new AI Studio chat that BC picked, before 4:04 PM.
- G8.8 taken in this release: from 4:04 PM Gravehand art goes through `tools/nano_banana.py`, with no browser. A cloud chat uses the "Nano Banana art" workflow on GitHub (BC: "approve route"). The number card samples and the Pro number card pictures were made that way. They are not in this build.
- Rest is a campfire, Shrine is a candle on an altar and Boss is a crowned skull.
- Room names show only on rooms you may enter and on the boss. Sideways the name sits beside the room.
- A phone held sideways keeps its portrait size. The cards take the height there.
- Upright, Force x Fury = total sits on one line. The panel is short there.
- The hint line sideways sits between Sort and Play Hand.
- The gear and charm icons were captured at half size. They draw small.
- Each backdrop has a faint shape in the glass of its arch. The portrait covers it.
- No new controller button for the slots. A controller player reads the same text in Menu > Charms and gear.
- A tablet held sideways shows no charm names beside the slots. There is no room in the right column. Upright shows them.
- The deck stack is a little smaller sideways to make room for the slots.
- A charm's panel adds one line: "Slot N of 4. Charms fire left to right."
- Bosses carry a "Boss" tag beside the name, so the rank never reads by color alone.
- One older test step read the screen too early and failed now and then. It now waits for the monster to show. The game did not change for it.
