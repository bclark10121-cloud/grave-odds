# Gravehand v0.4.2

A new app icon. BC picked it on 2026-11-21: "make a new logo and apk for gravehand, dont like the curent one", then "B for apk image" and "use the same image on teh gameshelf". Nothing in the game itself changed.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over 0.4.1. The home screen icon is a black spade with a gold rim and a crowned skull with ruby eyes, on cobalt stained glass.
2. Look at the icon's shape on your phone (round or rounded square). The crown, the tip of the spade and its base are all inside the cut.
3. Open the game page. The icon at the top and the browser tab icon show the same picture.
4. Open The Game Shelf. The Gravehand card shows the same spade and skull, and reads Version 0.4.2.
5. Start the game. The start screen shows 0.4.2. Your run in progress and your records are still there.

## New in this version

- A new app icon in the game's stained glass style, made with Nano Banana Pro. It replaces the plain card drawn in code.
- The icon is fitted to Android's safe area, so a round cut keeps the whole spade.
- The Game Shelf card shows the same picture, in a wide cut.
- Version 0.4.2 (code 7).

## Full test checklist

1. `RULES check ok`, `SAVESTORE check ok`, `DAYONE check ok`, `COMMON check ok` and `SECRETS check ok`.
2. `ICONS ok` from the kit's icon tool, with the check that no other game uses this picture (G6.10).
3. `tests/web_day_one.py` and `tests/web_tap.py` on the 0.4.2 browser build. `errors []`.
4. The APK: `SIGN ok`, `BUILD check ok`, and the launcher icons read back out of the APK.
5. `release_check.py` and `page_check.py` on the live page.

## Look check

- The icon at 192, 96 and 48 px, cut round and as a rounded square: the spade, the crown and the two ruby eyes read at every size.
- The picture is the one BC picked, unchanged, minus its gold picture frame. The blue glass around it carries on along the rays to the edges.

## Not tested

- The icon on a real phone and in a real launcher's themed icon mode.
- Real devices, a real controller and sound by ear. The game did not change.
- No full end-to-end run (G7.9).

## Judgment calls

- The picked picture ran the spade close to its edges. Android shows only the middle two thirds of an icon, so the picture sits at 72 percent of the icon with the glass carried outward. A square icon (the game page, the browser tab) shows more blue glass around the spade than the picture BC picked from.
- The fit is done in code from BC's exact picture (`art_src/tools/fit_icon.py`). A redraw through the art workflow was also asked for. It had not come back after ten minutes, so it was not used.
- The gold picture frame from the option sheet is gone. A phone would cut it at the corners.
- The one-color icon for Android's themed icons stays a plain white spade.
- The Game Shelf card shows the icon picture and not gameplay. BC asked for that. It is override 6 (G2.7) in `STANDARDS_OVERRIDES.md`.
- The three pictures on the game page stay gameplay.
- 0.4.2 was going to be the number cards. They move to 0.4.3.
