# Gravehand v0.3.0

Deeper. A third floor with a third boss, all twenty charms, the full gear list, Dread signature lines, stars, daily goals and player levels. Still plain shapes. Monster art from Gemini comes next (BC, 2026-11-21).

## Five minute test

1. Open Menu > Progress. See your level, today's three goals, the nine stars and the two cosmetic picks. Back is at the top. B closes it.
2. Start a run on Dusk. Win a few fights and check that a charm pick now offers the new charms (Pilgrim's Boots, Whetstone, Tyrant's Bane and more).
3. Beat the Ossuary King and the Tollkeeper. Tallowgate now opens after both.
4. On floor 3, The Bell Vaults, meet the Vault Sexton, Tallow Choir, Gallows Heron and the Cinder Reliquary. The Carrion Clock's strike grows by 2 every hand, so kill it fast.
5. At the end of the run, look at the stars and XP you earned. Open Progress again.

## New in this version

- Floor 3, The Bell Vaults, with three monsters, the elite Cinder Reliquary and the boss The Carrion Clock.
- The ten other charms: Pilgrim's Boots, Whetstone, Matched Set, Tyrant's Bane, Thorned Crown, Scab Collector, Executioner's Tally, Hidden Sleeve, Smoke Vial and Death's Hourglass. A note shows when each one fires.
- Gear: Trine, Marching, Lowborn, Crowned and First Blood on weapons, Thick Hide and Braced on armor, a second suit and Steady on rings, Pilgrim and Lantern on talismans.
- Dread items carry a signature line: Reaper's Edge, Ossuary Mail, Echo and Last Lamp.
- A Dread item is promised by the last boss if none has come up all run.
- Stars (G4.18): three per floor for clearing it, beating an elite and reaching a damage target.
- Daily goals (G4.19): three a day from a list of 14. All three unlock a cosmetic. No streaks.
- Player level (G4.20): XP from fights, floors and goals. Each level unlocks a table color or a card edge color. Pick them in Progress.
- Records adds Level and Stars.

1. `RULES check ok`: every charm, gear line and signature line has a golden number, floor 3, the Clock, the town after floors 1 and 2, the run win, the pity rule, a 0.2 save loading, progress data, bot win rates.
2. `DAYONE check ok` (16).
3. `tests/web_day_one.py`: 30 checks with the pretend Xbox controller, including Menu > Progress sideways and upright, `errors []`.
4. `tests/web_tap.py`: mouse and touch pick one card each, `errors []`.
5. Balance over 200 whole runs per difficulty with a best-play bot: Candlelight 96, Dusk 70, Midnight 36 percent. Deaths spread over all three bosses.
6. Secrets, kit and loudness checks pass.
