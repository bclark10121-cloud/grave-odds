# Gravehand v0.2.1

A fix for BC's first play: cards would not select.

## Five minute test

1. On a phone or a touch screen, start a run, pick a room and tap three cards. Each tap lifts one card. Tap one again to drop it.
2. In a desktop browser, click cards with the mouse. Same result.
3. Start a new run. The difficulty panel fits its three buttons with no empty space below.

## New in this version

- Fixed: a tap on a card counted twice, once as a touch and once as the click the browser makes from it. The card was picked and dropped at once, so nothing seemed to select. Only the click counts now.
- Fixed: panels such as the difficulty choice were sometimes far taller than their buttons.
- New test `tests/web_tap.py`: picks cards by mouse click and by touch tap in the browser.
