# Grave Odds v0.4.4

Gravehand is now Grave Odds. This release adds the scoring feel BC picked on 2026-11-21: a run score plate, a full count-up, a stained glass fire flare on a kill, loot that falls as a glass shard and a game speed setting. It also brings new card corners, the rose window start screen, the Crypt glass sound effects and a controller way into the gear and charm slots.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page. The name reads Grave Odds. The old Gravehand link forwards here.
2. Install the APK over 0.4.3. It updates in place. Your slots, records and best score stay.
3. Open the game. The start screen is the rose window. The window breathes, the skull's eyes glow, a card lifts off the altar, candles flicker and embers rise. Gregorian Chant plays.
4. On a phone held upright, read the tagline under the title. A soft dark band sits behind it.
5. Start a run and enter a fight. The top bar shows a SCORE plate with BEST beside it.
6. Look at your hand. Each card has a small gold-framed corner with the rank and suit gem at the top left, and the same upside down at the bottom right. It sits on the frame's corner and only clips the very corner of the picture. Number cards show their gems across the whole card.
7. Pick a hand and play it. The panel counts up: base, rite, each card with a rising bell, gear Force, Fury, each charm, then the total. The total matches the preview you saw before you played.
8. Play another hand and tap, or press A, Space or X, during the count. It skips to the total.
9. Kill a monster. Stained glass fire runs around the panel, the portrait flashes white and the glass breaks with a sound. On the phone it buzzes.
10. Feel a hit. A small shake scaled to the damage, with no pause.
11. Win a fight with loot. A glass shard in the tier color falls with a light pillar and a chime, then the gear panel shows. A Dread piece dims the screen more and rings longer.
12. Open Settings. Game speed runs from 1x to 4x and starts at 2x. Try 1x and 4x.
13. Turn on Reduce motion. The count, flare, shake and shard calm down. Take a hit: your health bar no longer flashes white.
14. With a controller, press D-pad up in a fight. Focus moves into the gear slots. Left and right move, A opens one, B or down goes back to the hand.
15. Beat a run. The achievement now reads "Beat the Odds".

## New in this version

- New card corners (BC: "too big in the corner and covering the artwork", then picked the small corner index with a plain bold rank on the card decision page, 10:47 PM): a small index on the gold frame top left, mirrored bottom right. Court cards and Aces show their whole picture. Number card pips use the whole card.
- New name: Grave Odds (BC, 2026-11-21, 8:41 PM). New page link, the old link forwards. The app ID and save files stay, so installs update in place.
- Run score plate with BEST beside it in New Rocker, one flare when you pass your best (BC's pick).
- Full count-up with rising bell notes, about 2.5 seconds for five cards at 1x. Skip with a tap, A, Space or X.
- Stained glass fire kill flare.
- Light hit weight: a small shake and a white flash, no pause.
- Phone buzz on by default: a tick per card and a boom on a kill. Android only.
- Game speed 1x to 4x, starting at 2x.
- Glass shard loot reveal in the tier color. Dread dims more with a longer chime.
- Crypt glass sound effects (Kenney, CC0): pick, deal, discard, room bong, tally bell, iron hit and glass on a kill.
- Rose window start screen (BC's pick, scene A).
- Controller way into the gear and charm slots: D-pad up, Tab or G.
- The achievement for winning a run reads "Beat the Odds". Its id did not change, so earned ones stay earned.
- All effects follow Reduce motion. The white flash on your health bar when you take a hit now follows it too.
