# Gravehand v0.4.3

Dark gold cards, a hand that stays in order, a charm menu with pictures and the first picks from the look and sound page: a new title font, a new tagline and new fight music. BC asked for all of it on 2026-11-21.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The title is in New Rocker, a heavy gothic font, and the line under it reads "Deal death, one hand at a time."
2. Start a run and enter a fight. The fight music is new: Interloper.
3. Every card sits on a dark gold frame with a small skull top and bottom.
4. Look at the corner of each card. A dark plate holds a big gold rank and a suit gem: a pale steel spade, a red heart, a blue diamond or a green club.
5. Look at a number card. It shows gem pips for its count. A 7 has seven gems. No gem hides under the corner plate.
6. Look at a Jack, Queen, King or Ace. Its stained glass picture sits inside the gold frame.
7. Hold the phone upright. Eight cards overlap and every one still shows its rank and gem.
8. Look at the order. The hand runs high to low: A, K, Q, J, 10 and down.
9. Play a hand or discard. The new cards slot into place. The hand is still in order.
10. Press Sort. The hand groups by suit and stays that way for the run. A new run starts by rank again.
11. Win fights until a charm is offered. Each charm is a tile with its picture, name, kind, rarity and what it does. Tap a tile to take it.
12. Visit the Curio Stall in Tallowgate. The same tiles show a price. A charm you cannot afford is dimmed.
13. Fill all four charm slots, then take another. The new charm shows on top and your four charms show as tiles. Tap the one to replace.

## New in this version

- Dark gold cards for the whole deck (BC picked look C, then "move to dark gold" for the face cards).
- Gem pips on the number cards, 2 to 10, made with Nano Banana Pro.
- A corner plate on every card with the rank in gold and the suit as a gem. Suits read by shape and by color.
- With Four-color deck off, diamonds are red gems and clubs are steel gems.
- The cursor card has a bright bone ring. The best hand hint keeps its green ring.
- The hand stays sorted after every draw. It starts by rank, high to low.
- A charm menu with pictures: tiles for the charm reward, the Curio Stall and the "slots full" swap.
- Title font New Rocker on the start screen, the game page and The Game Shelf (BC's pick).
- Tagline "Deal death, one hand at a time." (BC's pick).
- Fight music Interloper by Kevin MacLeod (BC's pick). The start screen keeps Gregorian Chant.
- The Game Shelf card shows a fight with a hand picked (BC's pick).

## Judgment calls

- Rank order runs high to low, left to right, as the first deal did before. BC said "by card number". Low to high is a one-line change.
- The sort choice holds for the run and is not saved. A new run and a restarted app start by rank.
- Pips for 2 to 9 sit in a 3 by 3 block under the corner plate. The 10 puts its tenth pip at the top right. Low cards look a little bare at the top right.
- All pips are the same size, 17 percent of the card's width.
- The corner plate is one width on every card, sized for "10". It is about half the card's width.
- The rank is gold on every suit, as in the sample. The gem carries the suit.
- The spade gem is pale steel. Black would vanish on a dark card.
- The card back did not change.
- Sorting only changes the order the hand is shown in. Two things read that order: the weak test bot and which of two equal best hands the hint picks. No score or rule depends on it.
- The Game Shelf card is back to a gameplay picture. BC: "the gameshelf image needs to be of the game, not the aplk icon". Override 6 from 0.4.2 has ended.
- The look and sound picks went on a picker page in the gravehand page repo, since the chat could not attach `bc-decisions`.
- Interloper is trimmed to 150 seconds with a fade, like Crypt was.
- The rose window start screen and the Crypt glass sounds come in 0.4.4. They need new code.
