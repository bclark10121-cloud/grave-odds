# Gravehand v0.2.0

The run. Two floors on the dungeon path, gear loot, charms, rites, shrines, rests, two bosses with twists and the town between the floors. Still plain shapes. The stained glass art comes in 0.4.

## Five minute test

1. Start a new run on Dusk. The floor 1 map shows: six rows of rooms, then the boss. The boss's twist reads at the top.
2. Pick a room with the buttons at the bottom. Win the fight. Look at the gold, the heal and the gear card: Equip or Scrap.
3. After your second fight, pick a charm. Watch the preview change when it applies.
4. Try a Treasure room (pick one of three items) and a Rest (heal or hone an item).
5. Beat the Ossuary King. Its plate eats the first 40 damage of every hand, so small hands bounce off. Take its gear, charm and rite.
6. In Tallowgate, visit the Smith, the Mender, the Curio Stall and the Chapel. Then Go deeper.
7. On floor 2, beat the Tollkeeper. Every discard costs 4 health there.
8. Open Menu > Charms and gear. Move a charm to the left. Close the app mid-fight and open it again: the same hand waits.

## New in this version

- The dungeon map: Fight, Elite, Shrine, Treasure, Rest and Boss rooms. Every route has at most one shrine, one treasure and one rest. One route always avoids elites.
- Two floors: The Crypt and The Drowned Nave, eight monsters, two elites and two bosses.
- Bosses: the Ossuary King (bone plate eats 40 damage per hand) and the Tollkeeper (discards cost 4 health). Bosses strike twice as hard every third hand, with a countdown.
- Gear: Blade, Hauberk, Ring and Talisman in four tiers (Worn, Etched, Gilt, Dread). Each shows its word, its pips and its color. Gear adds Force, armor, health, discards and gold. Never Fury.
- Ten charms that work through health, the monster, gear and kills. Four slots. Order matters. Reorder under Menu > Charms and gear.
- Rites raise one hand type for the run.
- Three shrines: Blood Altar, Offering Bowl and Whispering Skull.
- Tallowgate: the Smith (three items, sealed crates, restock), the Mender, the Curio Stall and the Chapel (rites and burning cards).
- The Codex (percent on the Records page) and the full ten achievements from the plan.
- Saves hold the whole run. Close and reopen at any point.

## Judgment calls

- Floor numbers were tuned by bot runs, not by the plan's table. Floor 1 fights are 480 to 620 health and floor 2 fights 1,100 to 1,550.
- The town comes after the floor 1 boss only. Floor 3 comes in 0.3.
- Gear compare shows the tier change and both sets of lines. Line by line arrows come with the art pass.
- The bot leaves about 200 gold unspent per run. A person who spends it will do better than the bot.
