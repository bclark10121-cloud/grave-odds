# Grave Odds v0.4.5

New number cards. BC found the 2 to 10 cards hard to read: small gems on a dark window that blended in. On the decision page he picked Suit glass with tracery. Each suit now has its own pale stained glass window, and the gems are bigger with a dark edge. The face cards and Aces do not change.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over 0.4.4. It updates in place and keeps your saves.
2. Start a run and enter a fight.
3. Look at the number cards in your hand. Spades sit on pale silver glass, hearts on rose, diamonds on ice blue and clubs on mint, each with rose window tracery.
4. Count the gems on a 9 and a 10. Each gem has a dark edge and reads against the glass.
5. Hold the phone upright. With eight cards overlapped, the corner rank still reads and the glass color shows the suit.
6. Play a hand. The small cards in the count-up row show the glass too.
7. Turn off Four-color deck in Settings. Diamonds turn red and clubs steel. The glass still follows the suit.
8. Check a King, Queen, Jack and Ace. They look the same as 0.4.4.

## Judgment calls

- With red-green colorblindness the club gems turn grey on the mint glass and read softer than the other suits. Shapes still tell them apart.
- The small cards in the count-up row keep their small pips (0.08 of the card width). The glass behind them is now smooth.
- Pro drew rose window tracery on all four windows, not a different motif per suit. Kept as BC saw it on the decision page.
- The pip edge drops 1.5 percent of the card width, a little more than the mock-up, which used a blur. It reads the same.
- The three page pictures and the Game Shelf picture are new 0.4.5 gameplay. The Shelf keeps BC's pick from 0.4.4, the Chapel Ghoul with a Full House, retaken on the new cards.
