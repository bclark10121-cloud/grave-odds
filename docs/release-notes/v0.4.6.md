# Grave Odds v0.4.6

The fun check build. Bigger cards you can size, buttons beside the hand on wide screens, a run summary to copy after each run, Xbox badges on the fight buttons and a longer win moment where the monster's glass shatters. No new rules or monsters. BC asked for it on 2026-11-25 and 2026-11-27.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game on your laptop. The cards in the hand are about a third bigger than in 0.4.5.
2. Open Settings. Card size has Compact, Auto and Large. Compact is the 0.4.5 size. Large lets the cards overlap like a held hand, with the picked card on top.
3. On a wide screen, Discard and Sort sit to the left of the hand and Play Hand to the right.
4. With an Xbox controller, the fight buttons show X Play Hand, Y Discard and LB RB Sort. With a keyboard they show key caps. With touch they show no badge.
5. Win a fight. Over about 6 seconds at game speed 2x, the portrait holds, cracks and shatters into glass. A "Slain" banner shows the monster's name, the damage counts up, then any overkill. Then the loot comes.
6. Tap, click or press A during the win moment. The first press jumps to the end. The second goes on to the loot.
7. Turn on Reduce motion. The win moment is a short fade with a still banner, about 2 seconds.
8. Lose a run or win one. The run end screen shows a run summary: run time, floor and room reached, fights won, hands played, best hand and seed.
9. Press Copy run summary. The button reads Copied and the text is on your clipboard. Paste it into chat.
10. In a browser with a controller, the copy can be refused, because a controller press does not count as a click. The button then reads Tap to copy and the summary shows in a box you can select by hand. It never says Copied when it did not copy.
11. Lose to a monster. The title reads "Slain by the dark".

## New in this version

- Card size setting: Compact, Auto (the default) and Large (BC, 2026-11-25).
- Auto makes the hand as big as the screen allows. At 1920x1080 it is 1.31x Compact.
- Buttons beside the hand on wide screens.
- A hidden run clock. It pauses in menus, panels and when the app is in the background. It never shows during play.
- Run summary and Copy run summary on the run end screen, for BC's 10-run fun check.
- Xbox badges and key caps on the fight buttons (G5.8, G5.14).
- A 6 second win moment at 2x (8 s at 1x, never under 4.5 s), with the kill flare ringing the monster portrait and the shards kept inside the monster card (BC, 2026-11-27).
- Readability fixes: a clear 0 on the score plate, the kill flare clear of the armor line, Text size Large grows the corner rank, the count-up cards' rank at 16 px or more, no shimmer on the small count-up cards, charm panels open at their final size, more contrast on silver spades and an edge cue on clubs.
- Old save samples in the test gate (G5.24). Saves from 0.4.5 load.
- Version 0.4.6, code 11. The game, the export and the project file all agree now (0.4.5 showed 0.4.4 inside the game).

## Known items

- The kit's `screen_fit.gd` puts Text size Large back to Normal after the screen turns. It affects all games, so it is proposed for the shared kit.
- The bot estimates a run at 8.3 min on Candlelight, 8.7 on Dusk and 7.5 on Midnight at 2x. The roadmap target is 25 to 35 minutes. Your real times from the fun check decide.
- The health bar builds a new style every frame (older than 0.4.6). Small fix later.
- The button badges show the default buttons even after a remap. The button mapper comes in 0.5.4.
- Kill flare embers stop at the top of the monster card.
- During the Slain banner a thin gold line from the monster card frame shows through the damage box. Fixed with the painted win moment in 0.4.7.
