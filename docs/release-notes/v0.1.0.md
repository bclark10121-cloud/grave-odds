# Gravehand v0.1.0

The first build: the gray box fight. Three monsters in a row in the Crypt. Play poker hands to hit them before they wear you down. Plain shapes for now. The stained glass art comes in 0.4.

## Five minute test

1. Press Play, pick slot 1, then pick Dusk. You get eight cards and the Crypt Rat with its next strike shown under it.
2. Tap two cards of the same rank. The right side shows "Pair" and the damage before you play it. Tap Play Hand. The rat flashes and its health drops. It strikes you back.
3. Pick a few weak cards and tap Discard. They swap for new ones and the rat does not strike.
4. Win the fight. You heal 10 and meet the Bone Warden. Then the Lantern Wight.
5. Turn your phone upright and sideways. The fight keeps going and every card stays readable.
6. Open the Menu. Try Records, How to play and Abandon run. Close the app mid-fight and open it again: the same hand waits for you.

## New in this version

- Fights: 8 cards in hand, play up to 5, 3 free discards per fight on Dusk. Damage is Force x Fury.
- The monster shows its next strike. From the 7th hand of a fight each strike hits harder.
- When the deck runs out, the used cards shuffle back in.
- Three monsters: Crypt Rat, Bone Warden and Lantern Wight. Heal 10 after each win. A lost fight ends the run.
- Candlelight, Dusk and Midnight, picked at the start of each run.
- Sort by rank or suit. Settings: Four-color deck (on) and Best hand hint (off).
- Ten achievements, six stats, score and best.
- Start screen: a crypt under a stained glass rose window, five glass cards fan open on the left. Title font Grenze Gotisch. Gregorian Chant on the start screen, Crypt in fights.
- Hints teach picking, playing and discarding the first time only.
- Upright and sideways, keyboard, touch and Xbox controller. Android back opens the Menu.

## Full test checklist

1. `RULES check ok`: 45 checks. Hand reader, both sample hands from the plan (68 and 280), fight rules, empty deck reshuffle, late strikes, heal on a win, run over on a loss, the same seed plays the same, a run saved mid-fight reads back the same, bad saves refused.
2. All 2,598,960 five-card hands counted right (`-- --full`).
3. Balance readout over 300 runs per difficulty with a best-play bot: Candlelight 100 percent wins, Dusk 96, Midnight 72. A bot that plays one card at a time never wins.
4. `DAYONE check ok`: 16 of 16. The headless bot cleared all three fights.
5. Browser test with the pretend Xbox controller: 14 of 14, `errors []`. Start screen, slot list, difficulty, moving between cards, picking, playing with X, discarding with Y, the Menu, then upright.
6. Loudness check: both tracks at -18 LUFS.

## Judgment calls

- Monster health and strikes were raised from the plan's floor 1 numbers. With the plan's numbers the bot won every fight in two hands and never lost health. New numbers: 480, 620 and 780 health.
- Achievements fit 0.1. The plan's list for the full game swaps in with 0.2.
